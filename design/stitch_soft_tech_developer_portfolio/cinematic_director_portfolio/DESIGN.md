## Brand & Style

This design system channels the atmospheric intensity and disciplined framing of a modern cinematic title sequence into a high-end creative engineering portfolio. The brand voice is visionary, technical, and precise, evoking the production quality of cinema and the structural clarity of modern creative software.

The visual style synthesizes **Dark Cinematic Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Depth**:
- **Filmic Staging**: Deep abyssal backgrounds layered with controlled volumetric glows, widescreen 21:9 framing, and deliberate horizontal pacing.
- **Precision Craft**: Razor-thin hairline borders, restrained micro-interactions, and pristine technical typography offset against expressive, wide display letterforms.
- **Editorial Lighting**: Warm amber kinetic cues slicing through a cool, desaturated nocturnal palette, drawing the eye like high-contrast anamorphic lens flares.

## Colors

The palette establishes an obsidian stage with directional chromatic accents:

- **Base & Canvas**: Deep Obsidian (`#0B1215`) anchors the background with an ultra-subtle radial perimeter vignette darkening to `#060A0C`.
- **Surfaces & Cards**: Midnight Slate (`#111C21`), delimited by a crisp hairline border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Elevated overlays and hover states step up to `#16242A`.
- **Text & Editorial Elements**: Primary text is rendered in High-Key Mist (`#E8EEF0`) for immediate legibility without piercing harshness. Secondary text and metadata rely on Cool Steely Slate (`#8FA3AA`).
- **Main Accent (Amber Orange - `#FF7A1A`)**: Reserved strictly for high-priority interactive touchpoints, primary CTAs, active pagination states, cursor highlights, and dramatic kinetic flourishes.
- **Secondary Accent (Teal - `#1FB5B0`)**: Dedicated to taxonomy, technical tags, interactive icons, badges, status indicators, and subtle backdrop radial glows (`radial-gradient(circle, rgba(31, 181, 176, 0.12) 0%, transparent 70%)`).
- **Functional States**: Success aligns with Teal (`#1FB5B0`), warning/alert with Amber (`#FF7A1A`), and destructive actions take a desaturated crimson (`#D64545`).

## Typography

Typography balances the geometric tension of an avant-garde studio screen title with utilitarian legibility:

- **Display & Headings (`Space Grotesk`)**: Bold, structural, and wide-tracked in uppercase accents. Titles should leverage tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) on display scales to feel compressed, authoritative, and cinematic.
- **Body & Narrative (`Inter`)**: Neutral, neutral-density grotesque engineered for screen readability against pitch-dark backdrops. Set with comfortable line heights to preserve breathing room between lines.
- **Labels & Micro-copy (`Space Grotesk`)**: Small caps, timestamps, reel indices, and technical tags use heightened tracking (`+0.08em` to `+0.12em`) to mirror film clapperboards and monitor telemetry.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column responsive fluid grid optimized for ultra-wide monitors and widescreen editorial formats:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, 1.5rem gutters, with generous 4rem outer canvas margins. Showcases, project banners, and video reels adhere to strict `21:9` or `16:9` cinematic aspect ratios.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8 columns, 1.25rem gutters, 2.5rem outer canvas margins. Multi-column cards collapse to 2-column arrangements.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 1rem gutters, 1.25rem margins. Layout stacks vertically with horizontal scroll snaps for film strip carousels.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Generous vertical spacing (`space-xl` and custom section steps up to 6rem–8rem) ensures individual projects stand alone like feature film frames without competing for optical focus.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through optical atmosphere, tonal stratification, and localized illumination rather than traditional drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - `Level 0 (Canvas)`: `#0B1215` with global subtle peripheral vignette.
  - `Level 1 (Cards & Media Trays)`: `#111C21` with a hairline stroke of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - `Level 2 (Overlays, Flyouts, Menus)`: `#16242A` backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and stroke `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Atmospheric Backdrops**: Ambient radial gradients of teal (`#1FB5B0` at 6%–12% opacity, blur radius 120px) bleed out from behind featured work, simulating ambient stage backlighting.
- **Active Bloom**: Interactive card hovers and focused CTAs project a concentrated amber flare (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(255, 122, 26, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp industrial hardware styling with tailored, ergonomic curves:

- **Cards, Modals & Media Containers**: Standardized to `14px` border-radius (`0.875rem`), presenting a sleek, high-definition monitor enclosure profile.
- **Interactive Badges & Tags**: Scaled down to `6px`–`8px` for a crisp, machined identity tag appearance.
- **Buttons**: Secondary buttons and standard inputs inherit the container radius (`14px`), while micro-chips, progress capsules, and primary pill-style action triggers take rounded-full finishes where visual momentum is required.
- **Proportions**: Media slots, preview thumbnails, and hero frames strictly follow widescreen cinematic proportions (`21:9` primary showcase banner, `16:9` project previews).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#FF7A1A`, text `#0B1215` (bold weight), border radius `14px`, padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover state increases brightness and applies an ambient amber drop glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(255, 122, 26, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary**: Surface `#111C21`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#E8EEF0`. Hover transition switches border to `rgba(31, 181, 176, 0.4)` and text to `#1FB5B0`.
- **Ghost / Text**: Transparent background, text `#8FA3AA`, inline icon offset. Hover transitions text to `#E8EEF0` with letter-spacing expansion.

### Cards & Project Frames
- **Container**: Fill `#111C21`, radius `14px`, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Aspect ratio locked to `21:9` (full hero) or `16:9` (grid item).
- **Hover Behavior**: Card border shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` with a slight 4px Y-axis lift and activation of the background ambient teal back-glow.
- **Inner Padding**: `1.5rem` to `2rem`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Technical Tags**: Monospaced/Space Grotesk typography (`10px`–`12px`, uppercase), background `rgba(31, 181, 176, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(31, 181, 176, 0.25)`, text `#1FB5B0`.
- **Status Indicator**: 6px pulsing dot (amber for in-progress/available, teal for production live) adjacent to uppercase tracking text.

### Form Inputs & Interactive Fields
- **Base State**: Background `#0B1215`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#E8EEF0`, radius `14px`, padding `0.875rem 1.25rem`.
- **Focus State**: Border `1px solid #FF7A1A`, outline none, subtle amber ring `0 0 0 1px #FF7A1A`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box size `18px`, radius `4px` (radios `50%`), background `#111C21`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- Checked state: Fill `#FF7A1A`, border `#FF7A1A`, inner glyph dark `#0B1215`.

### Lists & Index Rows
- Film-index style: Horizontal strip separated by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. Left column displays release index (e.g., `01 // 2024`), center displays project title in display typography, right displays stack tags and external link trigger.
- Hover expands row padding slightly and triggers an amber horizontal accent rule.

### Media Reel Player / Viewport
- Custom video scrub bars using a 2px track in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` with played progress illuminated in `#FF7A1A` and buffer states in `#1FB5B0`. Timecodes rendered in Space Grotesk `label-sm`.
